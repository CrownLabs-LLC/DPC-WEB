On a tasting date.

No app, account or paid membership required.

Enjoy a tasting once per person, per restaurant, per eligible day. Return visits on later tasting nights are welcome, and you can visit another participating restaurant.

  1. Bring your DPC glass.

    Dine at a participating restaurant on a tasting date during its normal dining hours. Need a glass? Your server can hand you one.

  2. Scan the restaurant's tasting QR.

    Enter your email to check in. The DPC news checkbox starts checked and is optional; you can uncheck it and still enjoy your tasting. No email verification is needed.

  3. Show your confirmation.

    Once check-in succeeds, show restaurant staff the “Enjoy your pour.” screen.

Phone or page not working? Ask restaurant staff for the paper check-in option.

No glass yet?

Pick up a complimentary DPC glass at a participating restaurant or another DPC pickup point, while supplies last. You don't need to dine to pick up a glass.

Dining on a tasting date? Scan only the restaurant's tasting QR. If you need a glass, your server can hand you one during the same dining visit. No separate glass pickup form is needed.
