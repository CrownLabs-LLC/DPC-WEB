THE GUEST

Regulars who come back.

Members pay DPC monthly to belong. They come in Sunday through Thursday, and before 4 PM on weekends, the hours you want to fill. They book ahead and bring guests. Four times a year a member can introduce someone new to your venue through The Introduction, a bonus Pour DPC covers, rung as a standard Pour, with you carding the guest at service.

THE OPERATIONS

You control your house.

You set your own capacity caps and blackout windows. The app handles redemption verification, so there is no staff training burden. Editorial features and venue reporting are part of your Founding Partnership, plus optional Boost Missions to drive specific windows.

NO PUBLIC PROMOTION. NO PERCENTAGE OF YOUR SALES.

Everything else on the check rings at full menu price and stays with you. Your menu never changes for any guest, and DPC never takes a percentage of your sales. Compare that to a coupon site that drops your price to the public and then bills you a percentage on top.

AT THE POINT OF SERVICE

A quick screen and an ID check.

Live in-app screen, every time.

Staff checks the live confirmation screen on the member’s device after they tap to redeem, including that the venue name on the screen matches yours. The screen shows for 45 seconds. Screenshots and recordings are never accepted.

Photo ID, every time.

The name on the ID matches the member’s DPC account. This protects you and the member.

Correct DPC POS button.

Ring the right Circle on every redemption so settlement is accurate.

Refusal of service stays with you.

You keep sole discretion to refuse service or decline a Pour for any lawful reason, consistent with your policy and the law.

App issues are handled by DPC support after the visit. Your team never processes a manual redemption.

FOUNDING PARTNERS

The first venues shape the chapter.

DPC opens with a small group of Founding Partners. We bring on about one partner venue for every sixteen members, and the founding member class is capped at 200. We keep the founding cohort small on purpose, so every Founding Partner gets real volume from the first month instead of a thin slice spread across too many houses. The first venues to commit lock in Founding Partner status and founding pricing for the term. After the cohort fills, venues join a waitlist and come online as the member base grows.

  • Founding-cohort volume from the first month
