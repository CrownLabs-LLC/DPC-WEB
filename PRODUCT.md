## Platform

## Scope of this record

September 24 update: this campaign now has two distinct flows. Universal glass
pickup at `/glass-pickup` collects no pickup location and has priority for the
September 25 newsletter. It opens as soon as ready, with no advertised start date. Four separate
restaurant tasting URLs retain the `/glass-comes-back/<restaurant>` pattern.
Pickup confirmation explains future tasting dates; tasting confirmation says to
enjoy the tasting. Same-visit pickup and tasting is allowed when the guest is
dining on an eligible tasting date, with both flows completed separately.
One pickup per email across the campaign; repeat submissions reopen confirmation.
Pickup remains available while glasses last after October 14. After the final
tasting date, confirmation says tastings ended. Pickup does not require dining.

This record captures the confirmed Glass Comes Back campaign addition. Existing
membership and partner surfaces retain their established product rules.

## Users

Guests dining at four participating downtown Livermore restaurants, restaurant
staff inspecting a confirmation, and the DPC team reviewing campaign results.
Nick runs campaign operations while Brandi travels beginning September 27.

## Product Purpose

Help guests return downtown with their DPC glass for a complimentary taste of
wine while dining. Measure participation and collect optional marketing contacts
through a lightweight browser experience.

## Operating Context

Guests scan a restaurant-specific card at the table. The guest supplies an email
without verification and can decline marketing. Staff visually inspect the
confirmation. A paper fallback serves guests without a working phone; DPC enters
those records afterward. DPC privately exports participation and contacts to CSV.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Participating restaurants: Demitri's Taverna, Swirl on the Square,
  Calamari Bistro & Bar, and L Campo.
- Eligible dates: September 29–30, October 6, and October 13–14, 2026.
  October 7 is excluded. Normal dining hours apply in Pacific time.
- Guests may pick up a glass and participate during the same visit while glasses
  last. Each guest may participate once per restaurant per eligible day.
- Email is required. Marketing is optional, initially prechecked, and saved
  preferences persist unless deliberately changed.
- No app download, guest account, automatic email, mailing-list sync, CMS,
  reimbursement, membership mutation, or Meta integration.
- The public event page is shareable by Livermore Downtown Inc., which is not the
  event organizer, host, or sponsor.

## Brand Commitments

Extend the existing Downtown Pour Collective website and assets. Keep the real
restaurant names, source offer, and established privacy controls. Never publish
membership counts or invent testimonials, attendance figures, or service claims.

## Evidence on Hand

The September 24 campaign interview and agreed brief establish the rules.
The LDI copy kit supplies the offer, restaurant list, contact information, and
organizer disclaimer. The existing website supplies its established identity and
partner logos. No photograph of the event's actual wine glass was supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make the offer and eligible dates easy to understand before the guest arrives.
- Keep the table-side task short and clear.
- Preserve guest choices and keep contact information private.
- Keep check-in counts honest about unverified email identity.
- Give Nick an operationally complete handoff before Brandi travels.
