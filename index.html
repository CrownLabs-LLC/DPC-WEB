  1. 01

    Choose your Circle.

    Choose Tap for beer, Cellar for wine or beer, or Reserve for cocktails, spirits, wine or beer. Enjoy eligible selections offered by your monthly venues.

  2. 02

    Find your next stop.

    After joining, follow your setup email. Your Pours appear once account setup is complete and your monthly venues are ready.

  3. 03

    Settle in. Say hello.

    When you’re ready to order at one of your monthly venues, confirm your Pour in the app. Show the live confirmation and your photo ID to the bartender, then enjoy your visit.

A few things to know.

When can I use my Pours?

During the venue’s operating hours, Sunday through Thursday any time, and Friday and Saturday strictly before 4 PM, Pacific time. Venue blackout dates can apply; check the app before you go.

Do unused Pours carry over?

No. Standard Pours expire at the end of your membership month. Your app shows your new Pours and their expiration dates.

What can I order?

Choose eligible standard drinks offered by your monthly venue: beer with Tap; wine or beer with Cellar; cocktails, spirits, wine or beer with Reserve. Options depend on your Circle and the venue’s program. Premium, top-shelf, vintage, and specialty selections are outside the program. Check your Pour in the app before you order.

Can I pause my membership?

Membership pause is not currently available. You can turn off renewal, or contact us if you need help with your membership.

How do I turn off renewal?

Use the subscription-management link in the app or contact support. Monthly access continues through the current paid period. Annual membership is prepaid for twelve months; turning off renewal preserves access through that paid term.
