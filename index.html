WHY THIS EXISTS

Downtown only stays great when the people who love it show up.

The restaurants, alehouses, wine bars, breweries, and wineries in downtown Livermore are run by people who chose this town. They live here. They hire locals. They sponsor little league teams and donate to school auctions. They are the reason downtown feels like downtown.

They also operate on margins that get tighter every year. Rent goes up. Suppliers raise prices. Staffing is harder than it has ever been. A slow Tuesday hurts. A slow month is a problem.

Downtown Pour Collective exists to send a steady flow of locals to these places during the quieter hours that need it most. A community of people who show up on purpose. Members get a curated experience. Venues get a guest worth keeping. Downtown gets the regulars it has always deserved.

HOW IT WORKS

Five Pours a month. One at each of your five spots.

Pick what you like to drink -- we call that your Circle. Each drink you order is a Pour, redeemed right in the app when you walk in.

01

WEEKNIGHTS ARE THE POINT

Use your Pours during each spot’s operating hours, any time Sunday through Thursday, or before 4 PM on Friday and Saturday. The calm hours when a bartender can actually treat you like a regular.

02

YOUR MONTH, YOUR PACE

Your founding membership starts the day you activate. Each membership month, a fresh set of Pours arrives. They reset monthly and do not carry forward. If something comes up, the Pause feature in the app lets you take a break.

03

STANDARD MENU, NO FINE PRINT

Pours redeem on standard menu items. Premium, top-shelf, vintage, and specialty selections sit outside the program, but they are always available at regular menu pricing.

04

MULTI-CIRCLE MEMBERS GET MORE

Each Circle you add brings its own five Pours a month. Two Circles equals ten Pours. Three Circles equals fifteen Pours. One refundable $49 deposit covers you whether you join one Circle or all three.

THE COASTER PASSPORT

36 collectible coasters. One commissioned piece of Livermore.

Every coaster in the set is a piece of a larger picture. Pick them up one visit at a time at the spots you already go, and by the end of the year all 36 fit together into a single commissioned artwork of Livermore, created by a Livermore artist from the turbines on the ridge, Lake Del Valle, the vineyards, Lizzie Fountain, and the Wine Trolley. Three new designs drop each month, so the picture builds a little more with every Pour you redeem.

This is what makes Downtown Pour Collective (DPC) different from a punch card.
