WHY MEMBERS JOIN

Walk in as a regular.

There is a version of going out downtown where the bartender sees you and starts your pour before you sit down. Where the room feels like yours because you have a seat in it. That is the version we sell.

Five Pours a month gives you a standing reason to show up at your spots. The app handles the redemption. The venue handles the welcome. Within a few weeks the places on your list start treating you like one of theirs.

Your membership carries Founding Member status, a Welcome Kit made only for the founding class, a rate locked for five years, and recognition at every DPC event after. The venues get regulars they can count on, which is worth more to an independent operator than any promotion, and that part takes care of itself.

IS THIS FOR YOU

We built this for a specific kind of person.

THIS IS FOR YOU IF

  • You already love going out in downtown Livermore and want to do more of it.
  • You want to feel like an insider at the places you visit.
  • Weeknights and early weekends are your sweet spot.
  • You would enjoy collecting a story tied to your own city.
  • You care about whether the places you love are still here in five years.

NOT FOR YOU IF

  • You are looking for a punch card or a discount app.
  • You only go out late on Friday and Saturday nights.
  • You prefer chain restaurants to independent operators.
  • You rarely make it out, and your Pours would go unused.

36 collectible coasters. One commissioned piece of Livermore.

Every coaster in the set is a piece of a larger picture. Pick them up one visit at a time at the spots you already go, and by the end of the year all 36 fit together into a single commissioned artwork of Livermore, created by a Livermore artist from the turbines on the ridge, Lake Del Valle, the vineyards, Lizzie Fountain, and the Wine Trolley. Three new designs drop each month, so the picture builds a little more with every Pour you redeem.

Included with every membership. Nothing extra.
