Plan a visit together

Enjoy your Pour by December 31, 2026, Pacific time.

  1. Choose a participating venue with your inviter. You must visit the same venue together. Your inviter needs active redemption access and an available regular monthly Membership Pour.
  2. Bring your photo ID. Venue staff will check that you’re 21 or older.
  3. Your inviter handles redemption. When the bartender is ready, your inviter uses the DPC app to redeem both Pours. You don’t need to download the app or redeem anything on this page.

When you can visit

Sunday–Thursday during venue opening hours. Friday and Saturday before 4 PM. Venue blackouts apply.

Your guest Pour is separate from your inviter’s monthly Pours. See the Introduction rules for details.

Your Introductions

Bring someone new to the Collective. Share your link, they claim it, and you go together. Their first drink is on us. You have two in total in this one-time Giving offering, through December 31.

Your link can be claimed once, by the first eligible person who confirms their email and completes the claim. Send it straight to the friend you have in mind. Copy this link and send it to your friend before leaving this page. You can also save it privately to send later.

The Introduction rules at a glance

This one-time Giving offering provides two Introductions per eligible member. Each guest may claim one Introduction and must never have been a DPC member when claiming. Guests must be 21 or older and verify their email.

Redeem with your original inviter at the same participating venue during the same visit. Your inviter must have active redemption access and use an available regular monthly Membership Pour. Birthday Pours and special grants do not qualify. Eligible DPC selections only. Sunday through Thursday any time the venue is open, Friday and Saturday before 4 PM. Venue blackouts apply.

Claim and redeem by the end of December 31, 2026, Pacific time. Unclaimed links and unused Introductions expire with no rollover. A claimed Introduction stays attached to the original inviter, including if you join DPC before the visit. Marketing consent is optional and separate.
