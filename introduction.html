Planning your visit

Use it by December 31.

  1. Pick one of our partner venues with your friend and go together. Their app shows which venues are available that day.
  2. Bring your photo ID. The venue will check it, the same as anyone ordering a drink.
  3. Your friend takes care of it at the bar. When you order, they pull up both drinks in their app for the bartender. You don't need to download anything or do anything on this page.

When you can go

Sunday through Thursday any time the venue is open. Friday and Saturday before 4pm.

Your drink is covered by us, not by your friend.

The rules, in short

Open to anyone 21 or older who has never been a Downtown Pour Collective member. One invitation per person, confirmed by email.

Go with the friend who invited you, to the same partner venue at the same time. Your drink is on us. Sunday through Thursday any time the venue is open, Friday and Saturday before 4pm. Venues can pause it for private events, and your friend's app will show that.

Claim it and use it by December 31, 2026, Pacific time. Unused invitations expire. If you join before your visit, you keep your invitation. Marketing emails are optional and separate.
