## 2.7 THE INTRODUCTION

For this one-time Giving offering, every eligible active paid or complimentary member receives two Introductions total through December 31, 2026, including eligible new members, Founders and annual members. Holding more than one Circle, changing Circles or rejoining does not increase or reset that allowance. Your friend’s drink is on the Collective and is separate from your monthly Membership Pours. This offering does not include recurring quarterly Introductions.

Share your personal invitation link from the app. The first eligible person to confirm their email and complete the claim becomes your guest. Your guest gives their name as it appears on their photo ID, confirms they are 21 or older, and agrees to the Introduction rules and the Privacy Policy. Once claimed, an Introduction cannot be moved to someone else. You can cancel an unclaimed link and share a new one.

Redeem it together. You and your guest must be at the same participating venue during the same visit. Open one of your available regular monthly Membership Pours (including flex selections), tap Use an Introduction, choose your guest and their eligible Circle offering and beverage category, and redeem both together on your device. You use one of your own Pours. Your guest's Pour is the Introduction. Birthday Pours and special grants cannot be paired with an Introduction.

Your guest may choose an eligible beverage from any Circle the venue honors. Standard redemption windows apply: Sunday through Thursday any time during venue operating hours, Friday and Saturday before 4:00 PM.

Claim and redeem by December 31, 2026, Pacific time. Unclaimed links and unused Introductions expire at midnight at the end of that day, with no rollover or automatic extension.

Your guest must be 21 or older and must present valid government-issued photo identification at the point of service. You are responsible for confirming your guest meets these requirements before you redeem. The partner venue retains sole discretion and responsibility to verify age and to refuse service. DPC does not verify the age, identity, or sobriety of guests, and assumes no liability for service decisions made by partner venues.

If your guest joins DPC before your visit, they keep the Introduction and you still redeem it together with the original inviter. Existing same-venue limits apply to the guest once they join. If you lose active membership redemption access, new claims and redemption pause while links and claims remain saved until December 31. They work again if your access returns before expiry.

Introductions are non-monetary program benefits with no cash value. They cannot be transferred and expire as described above. Temporary loss of the inviter’s access preserves the claim until that expiry. The referral program integrity rules in Terms of Service §11 apply.

Partner venues may decline an Introduction redemption for any of the reasons set out in Section 7, including if the guest appears intoxicated, lacks valid identification, is disruptive or unsafe, or if service would be unlawful or inconsistent with venue policy.
